Aven Notes Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Aven Notes is a simple note-taking application that allows users to create, edit, delete, and pin notes.

Information We Collect

Aven Notes does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information from users.

The application does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.

How Your Notes Are Stored

Notes created in Aven Notes are stored locally on your device. Your notes are not uploaded to or stored on servers operated by Aven Notes.

Because your notes are stored locally, deleting the application or clearing its application data may permanently remove your locally stored notes.

Third-Party Services

Aven Notes does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect information from users.

Children's Privacy

Aven Notes does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the privacy practices of Aven Notes change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe how the application handles information.
