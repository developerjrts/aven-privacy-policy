Aven Notes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Aven Notes is a notes and code-snippet management application that helps users capture ideas, organize reusable code, pin important content, and use AI-powered tools to work with text. The application is designed around local storage and does not require users to create an account.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Aven Notes handles, where information is stored, when information may be sent to third-party services, and how privacy is addressed when using the application.

1. Information We Handle

Aven Notes does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or physical address to use the application's core features.

Depending on the features you use, information handled by the application may include:

  • Notes, titles, and text you create or edit.
  • Code snippets, programming language selections, and related information.
  • Note settings, such as pinned status and Private Note information.
  • Text and instructions you submit to AI features.
  • Technical information processed by the backend or service providers when AI requests are made.

We do not sell users' personal information.

2. Local Storage and Regular Notes

Regular notes, code snippets, and related application data are stored locally on your device. Aven Notes does not require an account or routinely upload your entire notes collection to an Aven Notes account server.

Local storage means that your content remains on your device during ordinary use unless you choose a feature or action that sends selected content to an external service.

Uninstalling Aven Notes, clearing its application data, resetting your device, or losing access to your device may permanently remove locally stored content. Unless a separate backup or export exists, your notes may not be recoverable.

3. App Features and Data

Aven Notes provides features for creating, viewing, editing, deleting, searching, and pinning notes and code snippets. Information needed for these features is handled locally where applicable.

Aven Notes does not use your note content for advertising or sell your notes and code snippets to third parties.

4. Private Notes and Device Authentication

Private Notes provide an additional layer of protection for content that you explicitly mark as private.

Private Note content is intended to be encrypted for local storage. Access may require biometric authentication, such as fingerprint or face authentication, with the device's PIN, passcode, or other supported device authentication method available as a fallback where implemented.

Biometric authentication is handled by the device's operating system and authentication mechanisms. Aven Notes does not need to receive your fingerprint or face data to request device authentication.

Encryption and device authentication help protect private content but cannot guarantee absolute security. Protection also depends on the device, operating system, encryption-key handling, and the implementation of the feature.

If you choose to submit text from a Private Note to an AI feature, the selected text may be sent to the Aven Notes backend and the relevant AI provider for processing. Avoid submitting sensitive information unless you are comfortable with this processing.

5. AI Features and Third-Party Processing

AI Note Summarizer

The AI Note Summarizer processes selected note text to generate a concise summary, key points, and, where applicable, action items.

AI Note Writing

AI Note Writing helps users draft, develop, rewrite, or improve text. Text and instructions submitted for a writing request may be sent to the application's backend and an external AI service to generate a response.

How AI requests are processed

AI requests are sent to the Aven Notes backend, which communicates with the configured AI provider, such as Groq. Provider API credentials are intended to be kept on the server rather than embedded in the mobile app.

Only the information needed for a particular AI request should be sent. The selected text and instructions included in a request may nevertheless be processed outside your device.

Using an AI feature does not mean your entire local notes collection is automatically uploaded.

AI processing depends on third-party infrastructure. The AI provider may process request data under its own applicable privacy policy, service terms, and data retention practices. These practices may change over time.

Do not include passwords, payment details, confidential information, or other sensitive personal information in AI requests unless you have considered the associated privacy risks.

6. Internet Connectivity and Server Data

Local note-taking features use information stored on your device. AI features require an internet connection when they rely on the remote backend and an external AI provider.

When an AI request is made, the backend and service providers may process request information, connection metadata, and operational information needed to handle requests, maintain service security, and troubleshoot errors.

Information sent to an external AI provider is not subject to the same local-storage protections as notes that remain on your device.

The retention of request logs, error information, and other operational records depends on the backend hosting configuration and the services used. Do not assume that every AI request is immediately erased from all systems after a response is returned.

7. Advertising and Analytics

Aven Notes is a productivity application, not an advertising platform. We do not sell your notes or code snippets for advertising purposes.

Technical information may still be processed by the backend, hosting infrastructure, or AI providers when needed to deliver and maintain AI features.

8. Data Sharing

Aven Notes does not sell users' personal information. Regular notes and code snippets stored locally are not routinely shared with Aven Notes servers.

When you use AI features, the content needed to fulfill your request may be shared with the Aven Notes backend and its configured AI provider. Hosting and infrastructure providers may also process technical information as necessary to operate these services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of the service and its users.

9. Data Security

Aven Notes uses a local-first approach for notes and code snippets. Private Notes are intended to use encryption and device authentication as additional safeguards.

AI requests are sent to the configured backend over the network. Security also depends on the backend, hosting environment, AI provider, and their respective safeguards.

No storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their devices updated, use appropriate screen-lock protection, and avoid sharing sensitive content unnecessarily.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Notes and code snippets are generally retained on your device until you delete them or remove the application's data. You manage the content you create within the app.

Deleting a note from the app does not necessarily delete information that may already have been transmitted to an external AI provider or recorded in technical logs. Such information is subject to the applicable systems' retention and deletion practices.

If you have questions about an AI request or the handling of information by a service provider, you can contact us using the details below.

11. Children's Privacy

Aven Notes does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Users should avoid entering unnecessary personal or sensitive information into notes or AI requests. If you believe personal information has been provided to us inappropriately, please contact us.

12. Your Choices

  • Create, edit, and delete your local notes and code snippets.
  • Choose whether to use AI features. If you do not want selected text processed by an external AI provider, do not submit it to an AI feature.
  • Use Private Notes and the privacy controls supported by your version of the app.
  • Stop using the application or uninstall it, bearing in mind that this may permanently remove locally stored information.
