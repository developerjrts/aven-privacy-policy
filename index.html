Aven Notes Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Aven Notes is a note-taking application that allows users to create, view, edit, delete, pin, and manage their notes and snippets. The application is designed with local storage in mind.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Aven Notes handles, how that information is stored, and how your privacy is protected.

Information We Collect

Aven Notes does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or physical address to use the application.

Aven Notes does not intentionally collect or sell users' personal information.

Local Storage and Your Notes

Notes and related app data created in Aven Notes are stored locally on your device.

Your notes are not uploaded to or stored on servers operated by Aven Notes.

Because your notes are stored locally, uninstalling the application, clearing its application data, or resetting your device may permanently remove locally stored notes and other application data.

App Features and Data

Aven Notes provides features such as notes, snippets, search, pinning, editing, deletion, and application settings. Information required for these features is handled locally within the application where applicable.

Aven Notes does not use your note content for advertising, profiling, or selling information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Aven Notes does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect information from users.

If third-party services are introduced in a future version of the application, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

Children's Privacy

Aven Notes does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Data Security

Since notes are stored locally on the user's device, the security of the device, operating system, and device access controls also affects the protection of locally stored information. Users should keep their devices secured with appropriate device-level security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the privacy practices or data handling of Aven Notes change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe those changes. The updated policy will include a revised "Last updated" date.
