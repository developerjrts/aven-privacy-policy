Aven Notes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Aven Notes is a notes and code-snippet management application that helps users capture ideas, organize reusable code, pin important content, and use AI-powered tools to work with text. The application is designed around local storage and does not require users to create an account.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Aven Notes handles, where information is stored, when information may be sent to third-party services, and what users should understand about privacy and security when using the application.

1. Information We Handle

Aven Notes does not require users to provide their name, email address, phone number, or other personal details to create an account. There is no mandatory user login.

Depending on the features you use, information handled by the application may include:

  • Notes, titles, and text that you create or edit.
  • Code snippets, programming language selections, and related snippet information.
  • Note settings, such as pinned status and Private Note information.
  • Text and instructions that you submit to AI features.

We do not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or sell users' personal information.

2. Local Storage and Regular Notes

Regular notes, code snippets, and related application data are stored locally on your device. Aven Notes does not require an account or routinely upload your entire notes collection to an Aven Notes account server.

Local storage means that your content remains on your device during ordinary use unless you choose a feature or action that sends selected content to an external service.

Uninstalling Aven Notes, clearing its application data, resetting your device, or losing access to your device may permanently remove locally stored content. Unless a separate backup or export has been created, your notes may not be recoverable.

3. Private Notes and Device Authentication

Aven Notes includes a Private Notes feature intended to provide an additional layer of protection for content that you mark as private.

Private Note content is designed to be encrypted for local storage. Access may require biometric authentication, such as fingerprint or face authentication, with the device's supported PIN, passcode, or other configured device authentication method serving as a fallback where supported.

Device authentication is used to control access to private content. Biometric information is handled by the device's operating system and authentication system; Aven Notes does not need to receive your fingerprint or face data.

Encryption and authentication reduce the risk of unauthorized access but cannot guarantee absolute security. Protection also depends on the device, operating system, key management, and implementation of the feature.

If you submit text from a Private Note to an AI feature, the selected text may be transmitted to the application's backend and the relevant AI provider for processing. Do not submit sensitive information to an AI feature unless you are comfortable with that processing.

4. AI Features and Third-Party Processing

AI Note Summarizer

The AI Note Summarizer processes selected note text to generate a concise summary, key points, and, where applicable, action items.

AI Note Writing

AI Note Writing helps users draft, develop, rewrite, or improve text. The text and instructions submitted for a writing request may be sent to the application's backend and an external AI service to generate a response.

How AI requests are processed

AI requests are sent to the Aven Notes backend, which communicates with the configured AI provider. The backend is designed to keep provider API credentials on the server rather than embedding those credentials in the mobile app.

Only the information needed for a particular AI request should be sent. However, the selected text and any instructions included in the request may be processed outside your device.

AI-generated responses are returned to the application. Using an AI feature does not mean that your entire local notes collection is automatically uploaded.

AI processing depends on third-party infrastructure, including the configured AI provider, such as Groq. The provider may process request data under its own applicable privacy policy, service terms, and data retention practices. Those practices may change over time.

Do not include passwords, payment details, confidential information, or other sensitive personal information in AI requests unless you have considered the associated privacy risks.

5. Internet Connectivity and Server Data

Local note-taking features can use information stored on your device. AI features require an internet connection when they rely on the remote Aven Notes backend and an external AI provider.

When an AI request is made, the backend and service providers may technically process request information, connection metadata, and operational information needed to handle the request, maintain service security, and troubleshoot failures.

We do not claim that data sent to an external AI provider is subject to the same local-storage protections as notes that remain on your device. The provider's handling and retention of submitted data are governed by its applicable terms and policies.

The retention of request logs, error information, or other operational records depends on the backend hosting configuration and the services used. Do not assume that every AI request is immediately erased from all systems after a response is returned.

6. Advertising and Analytics

Aven Notes is designed as a productivity application, not an advertising platform. We do not sell your notes or code snippets for advertising purposes.

This policy does not represent that every library, hosting provider, or infrastructure component is incapable of processing technical information. Information handled by the AI backend or its providers is described in the AI processing sections above.

7. Data Sharing

Aven Notes does not sell users' personal information. Regular notes and code snippets stored locally are not routinely shared with Aven Notes servers.

When you use AI features, the content needed to fulfill your request may be shared with the Aven Notes backend and its configured AI provider. Infrastructure and hosting providers may also process technical information as necessary to operate these services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of the service and its users.

8. Data Security

Aven Notes uses a local-first approach for notes and code snippets. Private Notes are intended to use encryption and device authentication as additional safeguards.

AI requests are sent to the configured backend over the network. Security also depends on the backend, hosting environment, AI provider, and their respective safeguards.

No storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their devices updated, use appropriate screen-lock protection, and avoid sharing sensitive content unnecessarily.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Notes and code snippets are generally retained on your device until you delete them or remove the application data. You control the content you create and manage within the app.

Deleting a note from the app does not necessarily delete information that may already have been transmitted to an external AI provider or recorded in technical logs. Such information is subject to the applicable systems' retention and deletion practices.

If you have questions about a particular AI request or the handling of information by a service provider, you can contact us using the details below.

10. Children's Privacy

Aven Notes is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the application does not require an account, users should still avoid entering unnecessary personal or sensitive information into notes or AI requests. If you believe personal information has been provided to us inappropriately, please contact us.

11. Your Choices

  • You can create, edit, and delete your local notes and code snippets within the application.
  • You can choose whether to use AI features. If you do not want selected text processed by an external AI provider, do not submit it to an AI feature.
  • You can use Private Notes to protect supported content with the app's available privacy controls.
  • You can stop using the application and uninstall it, bearing in mind that this may permanently remove locally stored information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, data processing practices, service providers, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Please review this page periodically.
